# Where the Burrows Lead

Story development draft v1 · 2026-11-03 · branch `meerkat-story`

An original animated fantasy adventure developed as sixteen connected releases, each 80–100 seconds, assembling into a 24-minute film. Working title and new story details are proposals for creative review.

## The movie

When the ancient passages beneath their desert home begin rearranging themselves, an anxious young inventor and his impulsive childhood companion must lead their four-person group through a failing shelter network—while confronting the different futures hidden inside their promise to explore together.

The shelter network once adapted the settlement to the shifting desert. A well-intentioned repair blocked its western outlet. The next strong seasonal wind activates a protective sequence that now threatens occupied burrows. The group must uncover the pattern, help evacuate the affected homes, and give the system a safe new route.

The community survives. Some lower homes must be relinquished, and a new entrance opens toward the desert. Tavi and Sera choose a more honest version of their childhood promise. The central mystery and immediate danger are resolved within this film.

## Decisions established with the user

- Meerkat/suricate characters; soft cinematic 3D animation with realistic fur and expressive faces.
- Fantasy desert with ancient burrows; adventure, cute comedy, emotion, suspense and danger.
- Four principal characters, two boys and two girls. Tavi and Sera are the central duo.
- Tavi is male, 17; Sera is female, 18. Ages describe human-equivalent maturity.
- They are unrelated childhood companions raised in the same communal settlement.
- Tavi is anxious and inventive; Sera is bold and impulsive. They rely on cleverness in a magical world.
- English-speaking characters. Gentle romance with a K-drama influence and one brief mutual kiss.
- Their childhood promise to explore together now conflicts with their emerging ambitions.
- The changing burrows are an ancient protective system. The ending is a hopeful transformation.
- Releases last roughly one to two minutes; the final movie lasts 20–30 minutes.

Nima, Kesh, the supporting adult, place names, precise mechanism, specific incidents and all dialogue below are draft creative choices. They can be revised without changing the agreed foundation.

## Current deliverable

This package contains a complete treatment and chapter outline. It is not a finished screenplay or a generated film. Each chapter is a dramatic sequence containing multiple shots; it is not equivalent to the pipeline's current narration-based `Scene` object. The pipeline can render a **narrated adaptation** of each chapter as topic `meerkat_story` (see "Story chapters" in the root README); dialogue voices and identity conditioning remain open.

Review the characters, mechanism and ending before scripting and visual development. Once those are accepted, the first production milestone is consistent character turnarounds and a short two-character dialogue/acting test.
