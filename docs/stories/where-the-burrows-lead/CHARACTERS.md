# Character bible

Draft v1. Ages are human-equivalent maturity. All four principals are unrelated meerkats raised in the same communal burrow settlement. They have ordinary learned abilities; none can command the world's magic.

## Shared design language

Soft cinematic 3D animation, credible fur, clear silhouettes and expressive faces. Keep the long meerkat muzzle, small ears, dark eye patches, slender tail and recognizable animal paws. Alternate upright attention with low running and digging. Their bodies remain animal-shaped when gesturing or using tools.

Expressions use breathing, ears, paws, posture and tail as much as brows and mouth. Emotional close-ups need time to read. Softness comes from rounded facial forms and tactile fur; these teenage leads should still look their intended ages. Accessories are few, functional and consistently placed.

The height ratios below are initial model-sheet specifications, measured upright excluding ears. Tavi is the reference at 1.00. Colors are lighting references rather than an instruction to flatten the fur into solid patches.

Tavi has natural dark eye patches and a pale muzzle; Sera a pale throat; Nima rounded ears; Kesh a darker muzzle. All have warm dark-brown eyes with individual eyelid shapes. Tavi's left brow marking is fur, not a wound.

## Tavi

**An anxious inventor learning to speak for his own future.**

Seventeen, male. Compact through the torso with slightly oversized ears, an attentive forward tilt and a softer silhouette than Sera. A dusting of longer cheek fur catches warm light. His tail turns inward when he feels exposed and becomes a balancing tool when he concentrates on a mechanism.

Tavi likes the part of a broken object that still works. He carries reed sleeves, short cord, a small wooden probe and a folding bridge clamp he has been improving. His pouch is organized by the task he expects to perform, which makes an unexpected task briefly disastrous. He touches the pouch clasp before volunteering an idea.

He sees causes, constraints and moving parts. He can test a theory with simple materials and explain it through something another character can see. His limitation is the point at which he must act: he wants one more observation before choosing. Under social pressure he lists possibilities until the useful one becomes hard to hear.

Tavi wants to study the old network and become a maker whose work earns lasting trust. He also wants the journey he and Sera imagined. Those desires genuinely compete. He has quietly treated staying useful to others as a way of securing his place among them.

With Sera he is funny, dry and more relaxed than he is in a group. When a compliment becomes personal, he loses the fluency he has around machines. He remembers her preferences without announcing it: a tool grip wrapped to fit her paw, a fastening she can release quickly. His affection should feel reciprocal, with clear moments when she looks after him too.

His flawed early solution is to hold one changing passage still. He applies the same instinct to their promise: if he can preserve its original shape, he will not have to risk an honest conversation. His growth appears when he releases his own clamp, accepts an imperfect but testable plan, and states the work he wants to do.

**Performance:** quiet, warm English voice; a little quick when nervous, precise when absorbed. No perpetual stammer. He speaks differently with each friend. Let a breath and a choice precede his firmest lines. Comedy grows from excessive preparation and surprising competence, without making his anxiety the group's target.

**End state:** he proposes an adaptable routing plan, asks for the observations he needs, and chooses field work as part of becoming an engineer. He makes room for Sera's ambition while naming his own.

## Sera

**An adventurous young woman learning to share uncertainty.**

Eighteen, female. Lean, long-limbed, with an upright forward-leaning stance and a tail that helps her counterbalance turns. Her right ear's slight fold makes her silhouette identifiable in profile. Keep the muted teal utility wrap closed at her left hip except in specific story actions.

She judges a route quickly: where a paw can hold, whether a ledge will carry weight, where air is moving. She has excellent physical timing and an appetite for finding what lies around a corner. She can make frightened companions feel that the next step is possible.

Sera wants to become a route-finder beyond the settlement's familiar paths. She has prepared a small travel roll but delayed talking to Tavi. His opinion matters enough that she is afraid an honest conversation will become a request for permission—or reveal that he does not want to come.

When challenged, she makes a stronger claim than the evidence supports. Her jokes can turn defensive. Under fear, she busies her paws with someone else's strap or rope. She has learned to be the confident one and fears disappointing people by needing help.

The old map is folded inside her utility wrap. She still carries it because the promise matters to her; carrying it has become easier than discussing it. Tavi assumes she has outgrown it. She assumes he remembers it as an agreeable childhood game.

Her affection appears in attention: checking whether Tavi is still with the group, making space for him to finish a thought, remembering a private joke. As their attraction becomes conscious, a familiar touch can make her briefly shy. The story gives her ambitions room to exist alongside that affection.

Her early mistake is a route she calls safe before checking a moving joint. Later, at the western outlet, she sees the same warning and admits she needs another way. Asking for observations becomes a sign of confidence she has earned.

**Performance:** direct, clear English with lightly playful timing; energetic without sounding loud in every scene. Her most honest line should arrive without a joke attached. Physical comedy comes from confidence meeting a small, stubborn object, followed by the private annoyance of someone noticing.

**End state:** she abandons a dangerous shortcut, trusts the group's timing and chooses a first survey journey with a return plan she actually wants. She can say both "I want to go" and "I want you with me."

## Nima

**A patient observer learning to make her voice heard while evidence is still incomplete.**

Twelve, female. Compact and agile, with rounded ears, a light chin and a darker stripe across her upper back. Her right ankle cord is her only permanent accessory. Her stillness contrasts with Sera's motion. When genuinely delighted, her restrained face opens into a broad grin.

She listens to things others talk over: the hollow echo of a wall, a rhythm that has changed, the pause before someone answers. She notices physical patterns as well as inconsistencies in what people say. She enjoys being asked a real question and dislikes praise that avoids taking her answer seriously.

Nima wants a discovery that belongs to her. She can confuse being correct with being finished: once she has an explanation, she may dismiss a person too quickly. She also waits to be invited into a discussion, then feels invisible when the invitation never arrives.

Tavi gives her quiet space to investigate. She can tell him he is explaining the wrong part of a problem, and he usually listens. With Sera, affection takes the shape of specific objections. Kesh's concern for appearances makes him easy to tease, but she values that he actually remembers what she said.

Her key observation is that the three warning notes are followed by a quieter return tick. A gate is still completing its lock after the obvious sound ends. Her attention solves a physical problem established well before the climax. She uses acoustic tubes and ordinary hearing; she has no supernatural sense.

**Performance:** calm, concise English, with dry humor and a recognizably young voice. Avoid making every line a precocious put-down. Sometimes she is delighted, mistaken, tired or scared. She can ask for help with a difficult task.

**End state:** she interrupts the control sequence when the others are about to act too early, explains what she hears, and begins a proper observation record with room for corrections.

## Kesh

**A capable organizer learning what leadership feels like without applause.**

Fifteen, male. Broad through the shoulders relative to the others, with groomed golden-brown fur and a pale patch beneath his right eye. He stands carefully upright when presenting an idea. Pressure gradually ruins his immaculate grooming.

His route cloth belongs in the loop at his left hip. He knows exactly how much rope and water the group has. He remembers gradients, return distances and whether a loaded route is usable by someone smaller or slower. His physical endurance supports these learned skills.

Kesh wants to be treated as a future leader. He notices when Sera gets the praise for a spectacular moment after he did the preparation. That resentment sometimes turns a useful comment into a performance. He can become rigid when a plan stops resembling the version he packed for.

He and Sera provoke one another because both want to be competent in public. Their eventual trust is practical: she accepts his brake arrangement; he follows her route change. With Tavi, he enjoys understanding why a device works. With Nima, he seeks the opinion he pretends to find irritating.

His most important choice happens during evacuation. He sets aside his neatly plotted route to help move residents and supplies along a less impressive but usable alternative. Later he repurposes the route cloth as a soft pad protecting a rope from a sharp anchor. The object remains torn and dusty in the epilogue.

**Performance:** measured, slightly formal English that relaxes around friends. No adult military voice. His comedy comes from wounded dignity, a good plan with an undignified execution, and his own awareness that the others saw it.

**End state:** he organizes people by what they need, builds a controlled release at the western outlet and earns responsibility through useful decisions. He still enjoys recognition; he no longer waits for it before acting.

## The promise and romance

As younger children, Tavi and Sera drew an imagined route on a small piece of cloth at the abandoned lookout. They promised to explore beyond the known dunes together when they were old enough. The map has a distinctive two-branch line and a triangular ink blot; it carries no readable writing. Its design must be identical whenever shown.

Tavi has built his expectations around a shared departure. Sera has started preparing without telling him. Both have real ambitions beyond the relationship. Their conflict is resolved through a conversation and a concrete choice about the next journey.

Romantic progression: easy familiarity → unexpected self-consciousness during practical care → the map's discovery and a hurt conversation → taking each other's judgment seriously under pressure → a plain admission of affection after safety → one brief, mutually initiated kiss. Give them room to choose the moment: eye contact, reciprocal approach, a short kiss and a familiar joke. Gentle K-drama influence comes from patient acting and remembered gestures. The kiss belongs to chapter 15 and occurs once.

## Mara, supporting adult

Adult female meerkat; settlement steward and experienced surface-weather watcher. Stockier silhouette, silver muzzle fur, a plain ochre shoulder cloth. Her voice is grounded and matter-of-fact. She knows recent repairs and evacuation paths; she does not know how every ancient mechanism works.

Mara listens when the group brings specific evidence. She organizes residents, keeps them away from moving passages and remains useful throughout the emergency. She recalls the western repair when shown its matching material. The group discovers an overlooked connection across old and new structures; the adults contribute the knowledge and labor needed to act on it.

One recurring adult speaking role and a small pool of background residents keep the settlement present without crowding a short film. No character dies in this draft. The loss is an inhabited part of the settlement and the certainty of its old arrangement.
