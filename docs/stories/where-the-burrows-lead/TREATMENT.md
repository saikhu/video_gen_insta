# Where the Burrows Lead — full treatment

## A door in the wrong place — chapters 1–4

Tavi is repairing a small model of a turning junction when three low notes pass through the wall. A reed sleeve rolls off his bench toward the workshop doorway. He follows it into the familiar turning gallery—and finds an opening where a wall should be. Beyond it lies a dark passage breathing cool air.

Sera appears behind him. She has found the same strange opening from another direction and is delighted. Tavi begins listing reasons to stand back. A quiet tick comes from the stone, late enough that neither gives it much attention. A small domestic interruption—a resident finding their shortcut has delivered them back to the same basket—lets the event initially feel curious rather than catastrophic.

Sunhollow is busy around them. Mara checks the strengthening wind, Kesh repacks a communal supply bundle with excessive care, and Nima listens near a wall where the three notes have faded. Pale repair mortar and ochre cord hold up a recently patched shade fitting. These are ordinary materials here.

Sera has a small travel roll tucked beside her usual utility wrap. Tavi notices it and assumes she has an errand. She almost corrects him, then lets the moment pass. Her paw briefly checks a folded piece of cloth inside the wrap. An insert shows part of its two-branch line, without revealing why she carries it.

The basin in the court begins to drain. Tavi and Sera return to the turning gallery. Its wedge marks match those on his model. The three notes sound again; Sera steps toward the opening as the third fades. The sleeve moves a little farther before the return tick. She pulls clear, acquiring a dusty scrape on her left utility wrap. Her confidence is shaken for a moment before a joke covers it.

Mara closes the changing route to ordinary traffic. The upper refuge and its dry ramp remain accessible. With residents to account for and an approaching storm wind, she needs evidence about where the water has gone. Tavi identifies a fixed maintenance rim he can inspect. Sera offers to lead along it. Nima has heard an extra sound after the obvious warning; Kesh brings the rope, route cloth and water they will need.

Their small party forms around a specific task: trace the diverted water and return with a usable route. At a maintenance alcove, Nima speaks into one test port and Kesh hears her through another. A shared laugh at the unexpectedly amplified reply establishes the tubes as part of the old network. Kesh marks their return path.

## A solution that makes things worse — chapters 5–8

The fixed rim brings them to a distribution chamber whose sunstone ribs hold a diminishing amber glow. A relief of shaped channels lights in sections as water moves below. Tavi recognizes the same three alignments as his little model. He can demonstrate a local gate, but the full network is larger than anything he has repaired.

An opening briefly reveals the old water gallery: tiered stone, suspended mineral light and a thread of water crossing a vast blue reflection. The group falls quiet. For a moment the journey feels like the one Tavi and Sera imagined as children. Sera steadies him while he looks down; both become unusually conscious of the familiar touch.

The chamber gives them partial information. Something is building pressure toward the western side, and water is being diverted from Sunhollow. Tavi believes holding the local turning sleeve might stabilize the arrangement. On his small model, a clamp does exactly that. Sera presses for a practical answer before another route closes. Together they choose to try it.

They return to the turning gallery. Tavi fits his folding clamp to an accessible linkage, outside the moving doorway. The local motion stops. For several seconds they have an apparent success. He allows himself a proud smile; she returns it. In the background, a neighboring seam brightens.

Water surges into the old gallery. Its shaped channels catch the first flow, but a neighboring sleeve turns and divides the group across a broken maintenance crossing. Tavi and Kesh stand on one side, Sera and Nima on the other. Tavi's right side becomes wet in the splash; Kesh loses his carefully groomed appearance. Their return markers no longer describe an open route.

Sera starts toward a short crossing and then checks herself. Kesh finds a sound rope anchor. Tavi makes a small brace from what they have rather than trying to predict the whole mechanism. Nima listens. The third note fades; she waits for the quieter tick and points out the difference. The far sleeve finally seats. They cross along the fixed rim, testing each support. All four contribute to reuniting the group.

Nima watches water pass through the gallery's channels. It turns at deliberately shaped corners and avoids a fixed upper shelf. This is a sequence, not random destruction. Tavi realizes his clamp has transferred pressure rather than removed it. He wants to rush back and undo it. Sera asks where the released water would go while people still occupy the lower lane. They need a safe arrangement before they change the load again.

The old network is doing something purposeful. They have to understand that purpose.

## The promise they stopped discussing — chapters 9–11

Using a flow direction visible in the distribution relief, they inspect the western outlet through its lower service opening. The main shutter is sealed with pale mortar and ochre cord. The release lies above an unsafe moving section; simply striking the seal from below would leave the heavy shutter uncontrolled. Kesh recognizes the brake drum beside it.

They bring a loose repair fragment back to the chamber, where Mara has come to meet them. She remembers the grit problem and the western repair. The opening seemed unused, and residents had been protecting occupied space. Tavi connects that repair with the blocked relief route in the old design. Today’s wind has demanded a full cycle for the first time since the outlet was sealed.

The system was built to adapt the settlement to a changing desert. Homes have since spread into an old relief lane. With its western outlet blocked, the protective sequence is progressing toward a configuration that will put those homes in the water's path. Mara returns to organize the affected residents while the four work out whether another arrangement can preserve a dry refuge and water supply.

The group takes a short pause at the chamber's maintenance bench to sort equipment. Sera opens her wrap for a fastening. The childhood map falls out. Tavi recognizes the triangular blot before she can pick it up.

He thought she had thrown it away. She thought he had stopped taking it seriously. The travel roll becomes impossible to ignore. She admits she has been preparing to leave; he admits that he had assumed they would choose the day together.

The disagreement is intimate and specific. Sera wants to know what lies beyond the routes other people have named. Tavi wants to understand and repair the world he has already begun discovering underneath them. He also wants to go with her. She wants him with her, but has mistaken his caution for a decision to stay. Neither can honestly promise to want exactly what the other wants.

Nima and Kesh return from checking supplies. The distribution marker advances toward its final relief position. The conversation ends unfinished, but something useful has been said: the old plan no longer describes either person fully.

At the relief model, Tavi tries to reproduce the former configuration. Each safe western path still requires the lower relief lane. Preserving the whole previous arrangement leaves the new pressure without an exit. He has been asking the mechanism for the equivalent of an unchanged childhood promise.

He tests another route. It preserves the court, workshop, water access and upper refuge. It turns the lowest lane back into a relief channel and opens a dry upper connection toward the west. Houses and possessions must be moved from that lane first. The community will retain a home with a different shape.

Mara accepts the plan after checking the dry evacuation route and the model's predicted flow. She knows the residents it will displace and treats that cost seriously. The film shows her helping organize beds, tools and shared supplies for the move. This is a collective decision with work attached to it.

## Four kinds of courage — chapters 12–14

The four divide responsibilities. Tavi will operate the tested routing controls. Nima will monitor the sequence and its return tick. Sera will reach the western shutter release. Kesh will control the brake so the outlet can open without swinging violently under pressure. Mara will confirm that the lowest lane is empty before the final action.

Preparation is visible. Nima clears grit from the maintenance tube with a reed sleeve and tests the connection. Kesh protects the outlet rope from the sharp anchor with his route cloth; it tears and becomes a working pad. Sera checks the brake arrangement with him instead of assuming she understands it.

Mara's evacuation signal arrives through a runner at the distribution chamber: the lowest lane is clear and the dry ramp remains open. Only then do Tavi and Nima return briefly to the gallery, wait for the local completion tick and remove his clamp under the tested unload sequence. The neighboring seams ease. The clamp goes back into his right-hip pouch. They return to the distribution chamber while the outlet pair finish preparing. The storm wind builds, exterior light turns amber and the marker approaches its final relief position.

At the western outlet, Sera's direct foothold begins to rotate. She recognizes the worn motion arc that she dismissed earlier. This time she steps back. Through the maintenance tube she says she cannot see a safe way from that position and needs another view. Nima recalls the fixed sockets above the arc; Tavi describes their alignment. Sera and Kesh work out a longer reach using the rope and the stable wall. Asking for help costs time, but saves them from committing to the moving stone.

Sera reaches the release. Kesh takes the brake load and reports that he can control it. She lifts the final catch; he lets the shutter open in controlled steps. Air carries dust into the empty western wash. The pressure change allows the distribution interlock to complete its sequence. Tavi has his paw on the handle as its loud third note sounds. He looks to Nima rather than assuming it means go.

"Not yet," she says.

The quieter return tick follows. Nima identifies the completed interlock; Tavi commits the route he tested. Sera checks that the shutter is fully open while Kesh secures its brake.

Water fills the evacuated lower lane. The camera holds long enough to make the cost legible, then follows the higher dry route remaining open above it. At the court, the basin begins to fill again. The marker settles. An upper entrance, previously turned into solid-looking stone by the old alignment, opens toward the western light.

The group has solved the cause and the immediate danger. Their previous failures made the solution possible because they observed them, discussed them and changed their choices.

## A different map — chapters 15–16

After the wind begins to ease, Sera and Kesh return by the newly opened dry route. The four meet above the court. Mara is settling residents in temporary spaces. Familiar blankets and tools have survived the move. Someone sets the communal kettle down on a relocated bench. The place is changed and recognizably theirs.

Kesh examines his torn route cloth. Nima records the return tick and the outlet arrangement in a simple observation sketch, leaving space beside it for the next correction. They have tasks they want to continue, independent of the romance.

Tavi and Sera get a quiet moment beside the court. She produces the old map openly. He explains that he wants to join a first outward survey and learn from what they find. He also wants to return and help understand the network. She says that she wants to explore and choose where she goes, and that she wants him beside her on the first route.

They acknowledge how much they left unsaid. Familiar teasing softens into an honest admission of affection. Neither has to win an argument for them to choose each other. They hold eye contact, move closer reciprocally, and share one brief kiss. Afterwards he admits he did not prepare for that. Her answer—he prepares for everything—restores the easy private humor that made their bond appealing in the first place.

The next morning, Sunhollow is beginning to work with the new arrangement. Residents mark the submerged lane as a relief channel and start making proper homes in the safe upper spaces. Mara oversees the move. Nima adds a record socket beside the controls so the next group will inherit observations rather than guesses. Kesh starts a practical route board, using a patched corner of his cloth.

At the lookout, Tavi and Sera redraw their map. The old two-branch line and triangular blot remain visible. They add a first real route and a return path. The map acknowledges a shared journey and room for separate work.

The four meet at the new upper entrance for a short survey, with measured supplies and a clear return plan. Tavi brings a portable version of the model. Sera asks which path they should check first; the answer becomes a group decision. A small joke about the new shortcut recalls the opening domestic confusion.

The final view places their warm settlement behind them and the open desert ahead. A familiar low note sounds from the network. This time they recognize it, wait for the tick and continue along the stable path. The movie closes on understanding, affection and the beginning of a future they have chosen.
